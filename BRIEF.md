# Donna Project Brief

Strategy and positioning doc for the Donna marketing site. 
This is the working reference for every copy and design decision on this project — see the README for setup instructions.

---

## What this is

A portfolio project: a marketing site for a fictional AI-powered legal assistant, built to 
demonstrate B2B SaaS positioning, conversion-fucused copywriting, and interaction design.  
The goal of the site itself (in-world) is to generate booked demos from legal ops and GC buyers.

### Company: Donna AI

#### What they are
An AI-powered legal assistant platform for in-house legal teams and small-to-midsize law firms. 
Not a "replace your lawyers" pitch - positioned as the always-on second brain for legal ops: 
reviewing contracts, flagging risk, drafting from precedent, and surfacing anything buried in a company's
contract history.

#### Who they're selling to (buyer persona)
- **Primary buyer:** Heaf of Legal Ops/ General Counsel at a mid-market company (200-2000 employees) - not a Fortune-500
  with a 40-persoon legal department, not a solo founder either. This is where legal is stretched thin: 2-6 person legal
  team drowning in vendor contracts, NDAs, MSAs, and no bandwidth to review everything carefully.
- **Secondary buyer:** Managing partner at a small-to-medium law firm looking to increase throughput without adding headcount
- **Psychographics:** skeptical of AI hype (they've seen vendors overpromise before),
  protective of client/company confidentiality, value precision and accountability over speed alone,
  but is under real time pressure - the tension between "I don't fully trust this" and "I genuinely
  don't have time to review everything manually" is the core emotional hook.

#### What they're selling (the product from the company's POV)

- Contract review with clause-level risk flagging
- Drafting assistance grounded in the company's own precedent/playbook (not generic templates)
- Full-text search across a company's contract history
- Enterprise-grade security (this is non-negotiable messaging for legal buyers)
- Positioned explicitly as *augmentation* - Donna surfaces and drafts, humans decide.

#### What Donna (the company) wants this site to achieve

- Convert visits into booked demos - not free trials, not self-serve signup. Legal
  software sells through relationship and trust, so the funnel is deliberately high-touch
-Pre-qualify leads through the copy itself - by the time someone books a demo, they should
 already believe Donna understands their specific pain (manual redlining, missed clauses,
 slow turnaround), so the sales call starts from "convince me it works" rather than "explain what
 this even is"
- Build category trust for AI in legal specifically - a skeptical audience means the site has to do
  more trust-building than a typical B2B SaaS site would

#### Positioning
> Donna gives legal teams the judgement of their best paralegal and the speed of software - so nothing falls throught the cracks.
